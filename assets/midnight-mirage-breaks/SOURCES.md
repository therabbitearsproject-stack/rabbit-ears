# Midnight Mirage period movie television spots

All spots are preserved from start to finish, normalized to H.264/AAC with square pixels and an aspect-preserving 640×480 canvas. No advertising speech, footage or internal endings were removed. Original source downloads are hashed separately from normalized files. Every published file passed complete video and audio decoding with FFmpeg -xerror. Source dates identify the archived television spots; exact original transmission dates are generally unknown. Availability does not establish redistribution rights.
