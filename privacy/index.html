Privacy

For the website and the Rabbit-Ears.stream TV apps (Roku, Android TV, Fire TV). Watch-party details updated November 10, 2026.

The short version: watching does not require an account. Discord sign-in is optional and is used only if you want to leave public comments and reactions. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Optional Discord watch parties

If you sign in with Discord, we receive your Discord user ID and public display name. We do not request your email address, password, private messages or friend list. A secure sign-in cookie connects you to a session that expires after seven days; signing out removes that session. Discord access tokens are not retained after your identity is retrieved.

When you submit a comment or reaction, we store its text or emoji, your Discord user ID and display name, the channel, the episode identifier and filename, the playback position and the posting time. Your name and submission are public on the website and are posted by our bot in the matching Rabbit Ears Discord channel. Saved entries replay at the same scene on later airings; replays do not create new Discord posts.

Entries remain for later airings until removed. You can remove your own entries while signed in. Moderators can remove entries too. Removal hides the entry from future site playback and queues removal of the corresponding bot post in Discord. For help with removal or access, contact support@rabbit-ears.stream. Watch-party records and sessions are held in Cloudflare D1; Discord also processes original posts under its own policies.

What the website keeps

  • Who's watching counts. While a page is open it holds an anonymous, random placeholder in our database so the site can show how many people are on each channel. It's removed when you leave.
  • A visit counter. One number that goes up by one per new visitor.
  • Problem reports. If a tape, radio station or game won't play, or the page hits an error, the site logs what failed, a short error message and the browser family (for example "Chrome · phone") so it can be fixed. If you use "Report this tape", it records which tape and the reason you picked.
  • Visit statistics. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views, referring sites, countries and device types without cookies and without identifying visitors.

What stays on your device

Your last channel, volume, favorite stations and games, custom channels and similar settings are saved in your own browser or TV. They never leave it, and clearing your browser data (or uninstalling the app) removes them.

The TV apps

The apps download the channel schedule from rabbit-ears.stream and play videos straight from the Internet Archive. They remember your last channel on the TV itself and send nothing else to us. Your TV platform (Roku, Google or Amazon) may collect its own usage data under its own privacy policy.

Other services

  • Videos, radio stations and games are streamed from the Internet Archive and the Radio Browser directory, which receive your request the way any website you visit does.
  • Our database runs on Google Firebase, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare.

Children

Watching does not require personal information. Optional posting requires a Discord account and is subject to Discord's age requirements. Please do not include private or sensitive information in public comments.
